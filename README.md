# 🌍 Aero_Vision Forecast
### From Earth Data to Action — Predicting Cleaner, Safer Skies

**Aero_Vision Forecast** is an environmental awareness web platform developed for the **NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2025**. It uses NASA satellite data, air quality information, and weather updates to help people understand pollution levels and make informed daily decisions.

Our goal is to make environmental data easier to understand through a simple, user-friendly dashboard that presents Air Quality Index (AQI) values, pollution information, weather conditions, and health suggestions.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Air pollution is a serious environmental concern, but many people find it difficult to access, interpret, and understand air quality information.

When pollution data is complicated or not easily accessible, people may remain unaware of unhealthy environmental conditions and their possible effects on daily life.

Aero_Vision Forecast addresses this problem by presenting environmental information in a simple and understandable format.

## 💡 Our Solution

Aero_Vision Forecast transforms environmental data into an easy-to-understand web dashboard.

The platform aims to help users:

- Understand air quality through AQI values.
- View pollution-related information and maps.
- Access weather updates.
- Read simple health suggestions related to environmental conditions.
- Improve awareness of air pollution and environmental safety.

By making environmental information more accessible, the project encourages informed decisions and greater environmental responsibility.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Air Quality Information:** Presents AQI values to help users understand air quality.
- **Pollution Monitoring:** Provides pollution-related information and maps.
- **Weather Updates:** Displays weather information to support environmental awareness.
- **Health Suggestions:** Offers simple health tips related to air quality.
- **User-Friendly Dashboard:** Organizes environmental information in an accessible format.
- **Environmental Awareness:** Encourages students and communities to learn about pollution and cleaner air.
