# NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025

## About the Hackathon

The **NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2025** was a global, two-day hackathon held on October 4–5, 2025. Organised by NASA in collaboration with international space agency partners, the event brought together students, developers, scientists, designers, and innovators to solve real-world problems using science, technology, and open-source data.

The theme for 2025 was **"Learn, Launch, Lead"**, encouraging participants to explore new ideas, develop innovative solutions, and use scientific knowledge to make a positive impact.

## Objectives

- Encourage innovation through space science and technology.
- Use NASA's open data to address real-world challenges.
- Promote teamwork, creativity, and problem-solving.
- Develop practical solutions using programming, data analysis, and artificial intelligence.
- Inspire participants to contribute to a sustainable future on Earth and beyond.

## Key Areas

The hackathon featured challenges related to:

- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Earth Observation and Environmental Monitoring
- Climate Change and Weather
- Agriculture and Sustainability
- Space Exploration and Astronomy
- Data Science and Scientific Research

## How It Works

1. **Choose a Challenge:** Select a problem from the official challenge list.
2. **Explore the Data:** Research the problem and identify relevant scientific datasets.
3. **Develop a Solution:** Work with a team to build a prototype, application, model, or other innovative solution.
4. **Submit the Project:** Document the work and submit the solution through the official platform.
5. **Evaluation and Recognition:** Projects are evaluated, and selected teams may receive recognition through global award categories.

## Skills and Learning Outcomes

Participation in the NASA Space Apps Challenge helps develop:

- Programming and technical skills
- Data analysis and scientific thinking
- Teamwork and collaboration
- Creative problem-solving
- Project development and presentation skills
